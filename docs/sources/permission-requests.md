# Permission requests: drafts for the owner to send

Four first requests, in the order the research recommends. None has been sent. Each asks for the least that makes the listing possible, says what the site will and won't do, and offers removal on request. Fill in the bracketed parts, and keep every reply: a yes goes into `data/overrides/platforms.yaml` with its date.

Send by 4 December 2026 if you can. Fried Egg's first 2027 on-sale is 6 January and Rounds 4 Research opens in late April.

## 1. GCSAA, Rounds 4 Research

To: rounds4research@gcsaa.org

Subject: Listing Rounds 4 Research rounds on a free golf directory

> Hello,
>
> I run Golf Outing Finder ([site address]), a free directory of ways golfers can play courses they aren't members of. It lists charity outings and auction rounds, and links each one to the organizer's own page, where bidding and booking happen.
>
> I'd like to list the 2027 Rounds 4 Research rounds: the course, the state, the closing date and a link to the lot. I won't copy descriptions or photos, and I won't send any automated program to your bidding site.
>
> Could you either share the list of donated rounds when the auction opens, or tell me in writing that I may list them by hand with links? If you'd rather I link only to the main auction page, I'll do that.
>
> I'll take down any listing on request.
>
> Thank you,
> [name, email]
